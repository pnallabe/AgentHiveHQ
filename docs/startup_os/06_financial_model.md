## 9. Scenario Analysis

### Base Case (above)
- Win rate: 50% of pilots → paid
- Average sales cycle: 75 days
- Churn: 8–10%/year
- **Y3 ARR: $4.5M**

### Aggressive Case
- Win rate: 65%, faster enterprise adoption
- White-label partnership closes (nCino or Blend OEM) in Year 2
- CDFI Fund grant received ($500K non-dilutive in Y1)
- **Y3 ARR: $7.2M | Break-even: Month 26**

| ARR | $7.2M |

- First OCC pilot delayed 6 months
